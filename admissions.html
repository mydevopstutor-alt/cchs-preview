Continue your studies

Program interrupted? Let's look at your options.

If your studies were put on hold, book a free transcript review. An advisor will review your previous coursework and explain which CCHS programs fit, what any prior learning may count toward after assessment, and how you could pay — through a payment plan, Better Jobs Ontario, ODSP/OW or other funding if you qualify.

Credit for prior studies is decided case by case after assessment and is not guaranteed. Payment plan terms: to be confirmed.
