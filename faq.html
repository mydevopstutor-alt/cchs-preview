FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find yours? Call (416) 850-1456.

How much does a program cost?

Every program's domestic and international tuition is listed on our Tuition & Funding page, taken from the official CCHS program sheet.

When can I start?

Intake dates: to be confirmed by CCHS. Book a consult to hear the next start for your program.

Are classes in person or online?

Delivery format by program: to be confirmed. Our campus is at 705 Progress Ave, Scarborough.

Do you offer OSAP?

CCHS does not currently offer OSAP. Ask us about payment plans, Better Jobs Ontario, ODSP/OW, tax credits and other options on our funding page.

I started a program elsewhere. Can I continue here?

Book a free transcript review. An advisor will look at your previous studies. Any credit is decided after assessment and is not guaranteed.

Can I claim my tuition on my taxes?

CCHS is ESDC-certified, so eligible students may claim the federal tuition tax credit and the Canada Training Credit. Check the CRA rules for your situation.

Do you accept international students?

Yes, CCHS is a Designated Learning Institution (DLI #O19283878482). Study permits are issued by IRCC only; see our international page.

What is the Personal Support Worker program?

The NACC Personal Support Worker Certificate is 700 hours over 22 weeks. See details.
