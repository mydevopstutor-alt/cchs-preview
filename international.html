Study in Toronto

CCHS is a Designated Learning Institution (DLI #O19283878482), so it can accept international students holding a valid study permit.

Talk to an international advisor

1. Choose a program

All 25 programs list an international tuition fee. See fees.

2. Get your letter of acceptance

Apply and receive your offer from CCHS. Most applicants also need a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL).

3. Apply for your study permit

Apply directly with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). IRCC study permits ↗

Important to know

  • Immigration decisions are made only by IRCC. CCHS does not give immigration advice; consult IRCC or a licensed consultant (RCIC).
  • Work rules during and after studies depend on your program and IRCC policy. Check the official DLI list and post-graduation work permit rules before you apply.
